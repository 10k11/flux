# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour: being respectful and constructive, giving and gracefully accepting feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour: harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact details at https://lokii.dev. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
